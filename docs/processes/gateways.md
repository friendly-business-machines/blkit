# Gateways

> Branch and merge the flow of a process — exclusive, parallel, and inclusive
> routing — using blkit expressions on the outgoing flows.

## What this page will cover

Gateways control where a process goes next, splitting the flow down one or more
paths and later joining them back together.

- Exclusive, parallel, and inclusive gateways
- Writing flow conditions as blkit expressions
- Splitting and synchronising parallel paths
